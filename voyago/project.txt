### 5. The central creative concept: tell a story

The website must tell a continuous visual story rather than display a collection of disconnected sections.

The logo's person and camel crossing the desert provide the creative foundation.

The visitor's experience should feel like the beginning of a journey, discovering Morocco's landscapes and culture, exploring possible routes, hearing from real travelers, and eventually imagining their own adventure.

Build the homepage around this narrative:

**Chapter 1 — The Call of the Desert**

A cinematic introduction that inspires curiosity and creates an emotional connection with Morocco.

**Chapter 2 — A Land of Contrasts**

Discover Morocco's mountains, medinas, valleys, architecture, coastal towns, and desert landscapes.

**Chapter 3 — Choose Your Journey**

Present carefully designed tour cards that invite visitors to explore possible experiences.

**Chapter 4 — Follow the Road**

Show destinations and travel routes as connected experiences through Morocco.

**Chapter 5 — Stories from Real Travelers**

Display genuine Tripadvisor reviews to help visitors understand other travelers' experiences.

**Chapter 6 — Your Own Moroccan Story**

End with an inviting, cinematic call to action that encourages visitors to start planning their journey.

Carry this narrative through photography, typography, section transitions, route motifs, and subtle animations.

Do not use excessive text to explain the story. Let the photographs, layouts, and progression of the page communicate the feeling of travel.
